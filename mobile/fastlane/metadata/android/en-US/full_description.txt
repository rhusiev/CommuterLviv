A live map of Lviv's trams, trolleybuses and buses, and an honest answer to
"how long until mine gets here".

The app is a client. It talks to commuterlviv.r1a.nl by default, and to any other
server you or someone you trust runs if you give the sign-in screen a different
address. It does nothing on its own. There is no account here that is not an
account there, and there is no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

What it shows:

* Every vehicle on the routes you pick, moving on the map in real time
* An arrow only when the vehicle is actually moving. A bus standing at a light
  gets no arrow rather than a guessed one
* The next arrivals at any stop, predicted from how traffic is running today
  rather than from the printed timetable
* Stops you pin, with their times on one screen
* Five map styles, dark and light

The map is OpenStreetMap data rendered on the device from VersaTiles' free
vector tiles. No key, no account, no map vendor.

Permissions: internet, and nothing else. The app never asks for your location -
the map does not follow you, and does not need to.
