First release on F-Droid.

Live trams, trolleybuses and buses on a map of Lviv, with arrival times
predicted from how traffic is running today rather than from the timetable.

This version adds the door-to-door journey planner, search for addresses and
places rather than only stop names, saved places shown on the map, a traffic
view colouring each street by how far behind the vehicles on it are running,
and an arrow on a vehicle only when it is actually moving.
